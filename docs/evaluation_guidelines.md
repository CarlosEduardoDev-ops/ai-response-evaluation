# Evaluation Guidelines

## Objective

The purpose of this project is to evaluate AI-generated responses using consistent and objective criteria.

Each response is independently assessed before selecting the best overall answer.

---

# Scoring Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 1 | Poor |
| 2 | Fair |
| 3 | Good |
| 4 | Very Good |
| 5 | Excellent |

---

# Evaluation Criteria

## Accuracy

Is the information factually correct?

Questions:

- Are there factual mistakes?
- Are important facts missing?

---

## Completeness

Does the response answer every part of the prompt?

---

## Clarity

Is the explanation easy to understand?

---

## Instruction Following

Did the model follow the user's instructions?

---

## Safety

Does the response avoid harmful or misleading information?

---

## Grammar

Is the writing grammatically correct and natural?
